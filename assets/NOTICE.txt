Geography:

https://github.com/topojson/world-atlas
states.json: us-atlas 3, states-10m.json.
https://github.com/topojson/us-atlas
Underlying Natural Earth and U.S. Census geographic data are public domain.
These maps are used only for a fictional entertainment simulation.
All operational counts, trajectories and readiness events are fictional.

Sound:
Original four-cue orchestral music, composed in scripts/render-score.py and
rendered with FluidSynth / GeneralUser GS acoustic instrument samples.
See music/credits.txt and music/soundfont-license.txt for source and license.
Playback, crossfades, speech ducking and terminal effects use Web Audio in src/audio.js.
Electronic computer dialogue is rendered with eSpeak NG formant synthesis and
original signal processing; see voice/credits.txt and scripts/render-voice.py.
Only rendered voice clips are shipped, with no speech-engine runtime dependency.
Two short console effects (typing and command denial) are excerpted from the
user-supplied WarGames ending clip at their explicit request. See sfx/source.json
and sfx/NOTICE.txt for exact source ranges and processing. These excerpts may
retain background ambience. Music and Joshua dialogue are separately rendered
original assets. No video frames or film score tracks are included.

WarGames (1983) is the narrative inspiration. This independent fan game is not affiliated with the filmmakers or rights holders.
