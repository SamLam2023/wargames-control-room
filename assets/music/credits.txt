CONTROL ROOM — an original four-cue orchestral suite, composed for this game.
Intro / Gameplay / Crisis / Resolution. Composition source: scripts/render-score.py.
Instrumentation: acoustic piano, pizzicato and bowed strings, cello, double bass,
clarinet, French horn, harp, timpani, orchestral bass drum and tubular bells.
Rendered at 44.1 kHz stereo using FluidSynth and GeneralUser GS 2.0.3.

GeneralUser GS by S. Christian Collins:
