Original Joshua-style electronic dialogue rendered for this game.
Formant speech: eSpeak NG, generic en-us voice, monotone low pitch.
https://github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng
eSpeak NG is a GPL-3.0-or-later engine; no engine binaries or voice databases
are distributed with this website, only rendered speech from the listed text.

Original processing: terminal-speaker band limiting, restrained 38 Hz ring
modulation, short metallic echoes and quantization. Source: scripts/render-voice.py.
No extracted film audio, actor recording or actor voice model is used.
These clips are generic retro computer speech, not a reproduction of an actor.
